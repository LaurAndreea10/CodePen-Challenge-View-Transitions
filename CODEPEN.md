# CodePen publishing kit

## Title
Elsewhere — A Cinematic View Transition Atlas

## Description
Four destinations become one moving canvas. Click a place and the interface opens from that exact point as the image, title and index morph into a cinematic story layout. Explore day/night perspectives, a mini-map, progress timeline, split-screen comparison and optional generative soundscapes. Includes reduced-motion support and an accessible fallback.
