CodePen 2.0 Is Here

July 21, 2026·Chris Coyier·6 min read

After years of the same trusty editor, CodePen 2.0 is a full rewrite from the ground up. The thing you open every day is now faster, more flexible, and built around the reality of modern front-end work.

The headline change is real files and folders. No more juggling three fixed panes — organize a Pen the way you'd organize an actual project, with as many files as you need and a proper tree to navigate them.

The editor stopped being three boxes and started being a project.

Blocks let you compose reusable pieces and drop them into any Pen, while the new Omnibar puts search, navigation and commands a single keystroke away. It's the kind of quality-of-life upgrade that quietly changes how you work — you stop thinking about the tool and start thinking about the thing you're making.

Everything you loved is still here — instant previews, easy sharing, the same welcoming community. It just grew up to match the way front-end development actually happens now.
